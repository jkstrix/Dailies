Dailies Moments

Voice: checking where voice audio goes.

With AI on, voice audio from voice chat is sent to the Whisper address set in the Dailies settings. With AI off, nothing is sent anywhere. Players on the Privacy tab's list are never captured at all.

Ignored events are hidden from this page, from queries and from clips. Nothing is deleted: remove an entry and they come back.

Whose voice is left out

A player on this list is never captured for Moments and never transcribed. You still hear them while you play, and they are still in a recording's own voice chat when that setting is on; nothing of their voice reaches this log or any server. It takes effect at once. Recording other people's voices is yours to agree with them.

Where voice audio goes

With AI on, each voice segment is sent to the Whisper address set in the Dailies settings to be written down, and clip details go to the summary address. With AI off, which is how Dailies arrives, nothing is sent anywhere: who spoke and when is still logged, and the audio stays in the moments folder on this computer.

Token needed

This page is being read from another machine, so the game wants its token before it shows anything. It is the Remote Access Token (mcpToken) in the Dailies settings on the computer running the game. This browser will remember it.
